## Coverage limits

The Sony cards contain still images but no video clips, so this run does not verify Sony video/sidecar behavior on actual hardware. A single destination cannot verify two-physical-disk mirror/fill behavior. Windows/Linux builds, their hardware adapters, physical power-loss durability and camera-protocol transfer reliability were not established by this run. Metadata-derived shutter counts describe only the tested files and are not universal camera support.
